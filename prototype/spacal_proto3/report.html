spacal_proto3 — sPHENIX 2017 SPACAL prototype in solid_gemc

Last updated 2026-10-01 · log.md · result.md · SPACAL_PLAN.md

Overview

Port of the sPHENIX 2017 W/SciFi SPACAL prototype (PHG4SpacalPrototypeDetector, Prototype3 geometry XML) to solid_gemc Perl generators, plus a custom solid_spacal hit process that reproduces sPHENIX's fiber-level light model (Birks quenching, fiber transmission, light-guide efficiency, 0–60 ns window). Status: geometry and hit process built and validated; light sampling fraction agrees with sPHENIX's to 0.8% with sPHENIX's beam; energy scan 2–32 GeV gives 15.0%/√E ⊕ 8.2% with the full light model and 12.1%/√E ⊕ 1.7% without the light-guide/transmission maps.

With the default beam of sPHENIX's own macro, our light sampling fraction agrees with sPHENIX's to 0.8% (0.4σ). That beam is centred on the sector 1|2 crack, which its 0.7 cm spot partly averages over (sf 0.0145 within 2 mm of the crack, 0.0204 beyond 4 mm). For pencil beams the light-guide map moves the light sf by about ±6% with impact point. σ/E (sampling + photo-statistics only, trimmed): 4.9% at 8 GeV, 2.0% at 32 GeV.

Findings

  • The 8% constant term comes from the light-guide efficiency map, not the shower. At 32 GeV the same events give 2.8% without the maps and 8.3% with them. The aim point is the corner shared by four sub-towers, where the map varies most, so the 0.7 cm spot samples very different efficiencies. A quadratic correction in vertex position only reaches 5.1%; the map's structure is finer than that. A pencil beam at the same point gave 2.0% at 32 GeV.
  • Linearity: Erec/E falls from 1.07 (2 GeV) to 1.03 (32 GeV) with the full model, and from 1.09 to 1.05 with Birks only: about 4% is in the shower response itself, about 2% more from the maps. Cause not isolated; candidates are longitudinal leakage out of the ~15 cm module and deeper showers sitting further along the fiber-transmission map.
  • Low tail, 2–2.5% of events at 3–45% of E: electrons entering a fiber core almost parallel to it and travelling down the low-Z core without showering (e.g. 32 GeV events with a single fiber hit of 27–30 MeV, i.e. 13–15 cm of minimum-ionizing track in a 15.6 cm fiber). Real for a projective SPACAL fired at from the vertex; the core fits exclude these events.

Method

Erec = per-event sum / 0.0190134 (sPHENIX calibration); for “+ p.e.” each channel is Poisson-sampled at 500 p.e./GeV of beam energy. Peak position and width from an iterative ±2σ window with the analytic truncation correction (std inside ±2σ = 0.8796σ; recovers σ to 0.4% on synthetic data with a 5% tail); errors from 300 bootstrap resamples; resolution fit (σ/E)² = a²/E + b² by weighted linear least squares in 1/E. Script: analyze_scan.py.

Methodology

Simulation

sphenix_xml2param.py converts the sPHENIX geometry XML to GEMC parameters; solid_spacal.pl generates geometry, materials, hit and bank text files (variations Original, nofiber, mini). Fibers are validated against a Python port of the sPHENIX code, an independent transform chain, geantinos along fiber axes, Geant4 overlap checks and an analytic clearance check. The solid_spacal hit process (../../source/2.9/hitprocess/) makes one hit per fiber and outputs edep, Birks-quenched edep (kB = 0.07943 mm/MeV, the Geant4 10.7 value sPHENIX gets), and light = Σ(Birks × fiber transmission) × light-guide efficiency, with the sPHENIX maps from Prototype2Module.xml. Jobs run through run.sh.

Analysis

analyze_p2.py: per-event sums over all fibers divided by Ebeam; per-channel Poisson photo-electrons with sPHENIX's 500 p.e./GeV at sf 0.0190134; σ/E from a 2.5σ-trimmed rms. analyze_scan.py: energy-scan peak fits, resolution and linearity (method in the energy-scan section). check_hitprocess.py and test_spacal_model.C validate the hit process.

Open questions / next

todo Energy scan: tilt the beam a few degrees to suppress channeling, and aim at a sub-tower centre or correct for impact position to separate the light-guide constant term; isolate the 4–6% non-linearity (leakage vs transmission map). 2% momentum smearing not applied. The fiber-transmission map covers ±6.75 cm (Prototype2 fibers) while Prototype3 fibers reach ±8.2 cm; it is clamped, as in sPHENIX. Neutral-particle deposits are not Birks-quenched (G4EmSaturation quenches them via the electron range).
